What it does

Talk through your day

Record a thought, a meeting or a walk home. Life Wrapped keeps it, writes it down and helps you see the shape of your year.

Record in one tap

Long entries are split into short parts automatically, so they transcribe quickly.

Transcribed on your iPhone

Apple's Speech framework, set to on-device recognition only. Your audio is never uploaded.

Summaries your way

Basic, Smart and Smarter run on your iPhone. Smartest uses the OpenAI or Anthropic model you choose, with your own key.

Year Wrap

A year in review with highlights, challenges, people, places and the topics you kept coming back to.

Patterns over time

Streaks, when you record most, the words you use and summaries by day, week and month.

Export anytime

Take your journal with you as JSON, Markdown or PDF, or delete everything whenever you want.

Privacy

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your journal lives on your iPhone unless you choose otherwise.

Stays on your iPhone

Audio, transcripts and on-device summaries are stored locally and never sent to us.

No data collection

No analytics SDKs, crash reporters, ads or tracking of any kind.

Cloud only if you choose

Transcripts leave your iPhone only when you use Smartest, and only go to the provider you connect.
