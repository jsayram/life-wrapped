# Getting help

Thanks for using Life Wrapped. Here are answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Recording and transcription

**Why isn't my transcription accurate?**
Accuracy depends on a few things:

- Audio quality and how close you are to the microphone
- Background noise
- Speaking clearly and at a moderate pace
- Language and accent support

Try recording somewhere quiet and speaking clearly for the best results.

**Can I edit transcripts?**
Yes. Open a recording from History, tap Edit on a part of the transcript, make your changes and tap Save. Edited parts are marked Edited, and the Summary card offers to update the summary so it matches.

**Can I add notes to a recording?**
Yes. Tap the Notes box on a recording and type or dictate. Notes save as you type and stay separate from the recording's summary. They are used when your month summary is written, so with Cloud AI they go to your provider along with that month's summaries.

**I changed a recording from Work to Personal. What happens?**
The change applies to that recording right away. Its month summary is rebuilt to match the next time you open that month in Overview. If the recording is part of a Year Wrap, the wrap shows that it's out of date so you can regenerate it.

**The app said it couldn't hear me.**
If the microphone picks up almost no sound while you record, a warning appears under the waveform. After you stop, a recording with no voice in it is flagged, and you can delete it or keep it.

**Why are recordings split into parts?**
Life Wrapped splits recordings into short parts (30 to 300 seconds, set in Settings, then Recording) so they transcribe quickly and use less memory.

### Summaries

**Which summary quality should I use?**

- **Key Sentences**: picks out your most important sentences. Fast, free, always available, on your device
- **Offline AI**: a model you download once (about 2.3 GB, or about 1 GB on devices with 4 GB of memory). Free and private, on your device
- **Apple Intelligence**: built into your iPhone or iPad, needs iOS or iPadOS 26 or later on a supported model. Free and private
- **Cloud AI**: the best quality, using your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key. Unlocked with a one-time $2.99 purchase, with no subscription

**Which ones can make a Year Wrap?**
Apple Intelligence and Cloud AI. You pick one when you create the wrap, and it runs in the background while you use the app.

**What does "bring your own key" mean?**
You add your own API key from OpenAI or Anthropic and choose the model. You pay the provider directly and stay in control of your account.

**Is my data sent to the cloud?**
Only if you choose Cloud AI. Then text, never audio, goes to the provider you connect: transcripts for recording summaries, and summaries and notes for month summaries and Year Wrap. The [privacy policy](privacy) lists exactly what is sent. Transcription always happens on your device, and Key Sentences, Offline AI and Apple Intelligence keep everything on your device.

### Purchases and billing

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Go to Settings, then Purchases, and tap Restore purchases. The Cloud AI purchase screen has the same button.

**How do I redeem a promo code?**
Tap Cloud AI under Purchases in Settings, then tap Redeem code.

**Can I get a refund?**
All purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. To request a refund, visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

---

### Privacy and data

**Where is my data stored?**
Your recordings, transcripts and summaries are stored on your device. We never upload your data to our servers.

**How do I export my data?**
Go to Settings, then Data, then Export & backup. You can export everything or one year as JSON (transcripts and summaries), Markdown or PDF (summaries). Exports don't include audio.

**How do I delete all my data?**
Go to Settings, then Data, then Export & backup, and tap Delete all data. This also removes your saved API keys and the downloaded Offline AI model. You can delete a single year from the same screen.

## Troubleshooting

### The app isn't recording

1. Check microphone access in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, under Privacy & Security, then Microphone
2. Make sure no other app is using the microphone
3. Restart Life Wrapped

### Transcription isn't starting

1. Check speech recognition access in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, then Speech Recognition
2. Make sure your device has on-device speech recognition for your language (iOS may download it once over the internet)
3. Check that you have free storage space

### Offline AI summaries aren't working

1. Make sure the model finished downloading (Settings, then AI & Summaries). Keep Life Wrapped open until the download finishes
2. Check that you have enough free storage (about 2.3 GB, or about 1 GB on devices with 4 GB of memory)
3. Offline AI needs a device with at least 4 GB of memory
4. Once downloaded, the model works offline

### Cloud AI errors

1. Check that your API key is correct
2. Check that your API account has credit available
3. Check the model ID; tap View models to see the provider's current list
4. Tap Test in Settings, then AI & Summaries, to try the key and model together

## System requirements

- **iOS or iPadOS 18.0** or later
- **Apple Intelligence**: iOS or iPadOS 26 or later on a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in the Settings app
- **Offline AI**: a device with at least 4 GB of memory, and free storage for the model (about 2.3 GB, or about 1 GB on 4 GB devices)
